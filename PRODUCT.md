# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React, Vite and TypeScript for a maintainable company hub, deployed as static assets on Cloudflare Pages with Cloudflare Pages Functions for server-side inquiries. Content and destination URLs are configuration-driven so the three future business sites can be connected without redesigning the hub.

## Users

- Consumers looking for one of Xiongqi's three businesses: apparel, ceramics, or AI technology subscriptions.
- Business buyers and partners evaluating Hangzhou Xiongqi E-commerce Co., Ltd. and seeking a direct cooperation route.
- Future editors who need to maintain company positioning, business descriptions, and destination links without rebuilding the page structure.

## Product Purpose

The site is the corporate front door for Xiongqi. It establishes the parent brand, explains why the company operates three distinct businesses, and routes each visitor to the right independent site with minimal ambiguity.

Success means a first-time visitor can identify the company, understand the three business areas, choose a destination, or submit a cooperation inquiry within seconds.

## Positioning

Xiongqi is not a miscellaneous storefront. The hub connects three independently operated businesses through one company-level promise: practical products, honest information, and technology that lowers the cost of taking action.

## Operating Context

- The apparel, ceramics, and AI subscription businesses each have or will have their own website and content system.
- This first release is a routing and corporate credibility layer, not the final transaction system for any business.
- GitHub is the source of truth; Cloudflare builds and serves the production site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One company homepage with three clearly differentiated business portals.
- Company mission, principles, and cooperation information.
- Configurable external destination URLs for the three business sites.
- A server-side inquiry endpoint designed for Cloudflare Pages Functions and an optional D1 binding.
- No invented prices, inventory, customer logos, testimonials, certifications, or operating statistics.
- Chinese-first copy with a restrained English brand layer.

## Brand Commitments

- Company: 杭州熊奇电子商务有限公司.
- Parent brand: 熊奇; English expression Bear Up remains subject to trademark and domain verification.
- Primary slogan: 为自己，向未知出发。
- Mission: 帮助每个人从被动接受走向主动选择，把想法变成行动，把行动变成成长。
- Personality: clear-eyed without being harsh, determined without lecturing, pragmatic without cynicism, strong with warmth.

## Product Principles

- Route first: help visitors identify the right business before presenting detail.
- Separate the three businesses operationally while preserving one parent-brand standard.
- Earn trust with boundaries and specific explanations, not unsupported claims.
- Keep important content and links editable from small, documented data files.
- Build the hub as durable infrastructure for future products, content, and partnerships.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, reduced motion, and responsive layouts.
